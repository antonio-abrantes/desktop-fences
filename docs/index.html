The usual clones leave duplicates

DeskFrame, NoFences, and OpenFences float shortcuts in a window while the original icons stay on the desktop. You get two of everything.

We hide SysListView32 and paint over it

DesktopFences conceals the real Explorer icons, shows them in the fence, and restores them on drag-out, pause, or exit. Hide is never permanent.

What MVP 2 actually does

Several fences on one desktop. Same hide and restore as MVP 1 — the originals never stay duplicated.

Glass on the wallpaperDark translucent panel, 8px corners, sits behind ordinary windows.
Drag in, drag out, drag acrossSingle or multi-select from Explorer or between fences. Ghost follows the cursor. Arrow stays an arrow.
Move and snapDrag only from the ⋮⋮ grip. Let go near a screen edge or another fence and it magnets flush. Neighbors stay put.
As many fences as you needAdd and remove from Settings. Always at least one. Title left or centered.
Color the glassFill, border, header, and text per fence — or apply to all. Restore the default tint.
Portuguese or EnglishSystem, PT, or EN. Live switch. Names you already gave a fence stay put.
Tray, startup, layoutPause restores real icons. Start with Windows follows this .exe. layout.json remembers the rest.
